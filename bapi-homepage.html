Who we are

Bangladesh Association of Pharmaceutical Industries (BAPI) is the apex body representing the pharmaceutical companies of Bangladesh. Since its inception in 1972, BAPI has played a key role in developing the pharma sector to promote access to affordable drugs.

Working closely with industry and regulatory authorities, the association promotes good manufacturing practice (GMP) and its rightful implementation, to ensure the quality of medicines reaching patients — at home and in the 150+ countries Bangladesh now exports to.

One of Bangladesh's most advanced technology-driven sectors

Manufacturers here produce almost every category of medicine, including high-tech products like insulin, hormones, and anti-cancer treatments — meeting 98% of the local market's medicinal needs.

The industry also exports to 150+ countries, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and the EU, with companies actively expanding their reach into new export markets.

Expanding our global footprint

169.02 Mn USD

Export earnings continue to grow, crossing BDT 14 billion. The Bangladesh government has declared pharmaceuticals a "Thrust Sector," and around 1,200 products have received export registration over the past two years — reaching 150+ countries including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and the EU.

Competitive edge of Bangladesh pharma

  • The global generics market, valued at roughly US $1.06 trillion, is projected to reach $1.30 trillion.
  • Generic drugs continue to grow at a healthy, sustained rate worldwide.
  • Drugs worth more than $200 billion are coming off patent by 2025.
  • Rising healthcare costs, particularly medicine costs, remain a pressing global concern.
