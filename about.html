About BAPI

Representing Bangladesh's pharmaceutical manufacturers since 1972.

A member‑driven association working with regulators, ministries, and international bodies to strengthen the industry that supplies the country's medicines.

Mission

To promote a strong, quality‑driven, and internationally competitive pharmaceutical industry in Bangladesh — one that reliably meets domestic health needs and expands the country's presence in global medicine markets.

Objectives

  • Advocate for sound, evidence‑based pharmaceutical policy.
  • Promote adherence to GMP and international quality standards.
  • Support member companies on regulatory and trade matters.
  • Facilitate dialogue between industry, government, and public‑health stakeholders.
  • Encourage R&D, technology transfer, and workforce development.

Governed by

An elected Executive Committee, led by the President, and supported by a full‑time Secretariat headed by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Executive Director (ED).

Membership

Open to licensed pharmaceutical manufacturers operating in Bangladesh. Members receive policy briefings, technical guidance, and representation before regulatory bodies.
